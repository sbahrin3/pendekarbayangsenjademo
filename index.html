Privacy Policy

Effective date: 31 July, 2026

Thank you for using Pendekar Bayang Senja ("the App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you install and use the App. The App is designed as a simple, relaxing application for seniors.

1. Information Collection and Use

The App is designed to operate without collecting personal information. Specifically:

  • The App does not require registration, sign-in, or user accounts.
  • We do not collect, store, or share personally identifiable information (PII) such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, or payment information.
  • No analytics or tracking that identifies a user is used by the App.

2. Permissions

The App may request only the minimal permissions necessary to enable core features:

  • Storage — to load or save local media (if applicable) and to enable smoother performance.
  • Audio — to play in-app audio for features such as "Listen to Wisdom Quotes."

These permissions are used solely on the device and are not transmitted to any external server.

3. Data Security

Because the App does not collect or store personal data on external servers, there is minimal risk of personal data leakage from the App itself. All user activity remains on the user's device unless the user explicitly chooses to share content through other apps on their device (for example, using Android's share sheet).

4. Third-Party Services

The App does not use third-party SDKs or services that collect personally identifiable information. If future versions of the App include third-party services (for example, crash reporting or analytics), we will update this policy and disclose the services used.

5. Children’s Privacy

The App is suitable for general audiences, including seniors and families. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected personal information from a child, please contact us using the details below and we will promptly delete the information.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will update the Effective date above and, where possible, notify users within the App. Continued use of the App after any update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
